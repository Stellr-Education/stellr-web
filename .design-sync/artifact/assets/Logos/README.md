The Stellr Education logo, copied from the site's `public/images/`. Both SVGs are single-colour navy marks (`#0b439c`, with `#051535` in the wordmark) for light grounds such as `color-white` and `color-surface`; they cannot recolour, so don't place them on midnight. `logo-horiz-tight.svg` is the horizontal lockup for headers and documents; `logo-icon.svg` is the mark alone, for tight spaces and avatars. The two PNGs are raster copies of the horizontal lockup for email and places that can't take SVG.
